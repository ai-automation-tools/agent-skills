# Yearly News Montage

> Shape: **Montage** (single unified scene, landscape). Read `../SKILL.md` → *Shared conventions* first; this file holds only what's specific to the yearly montage.

Create a single landscape cartoon-style news montage summarizing the **defining U.S. news events of the previous calendar year**, then save, email, and publish it.

## Period & research
- Resolve the **previous full calendar year** (e.g. run in early 2027 → year **2026**); use that exact year throughout the run.
- Research the **ten to fourteen** biggest, most defining U.S. news stories of that year via web search. Choose the year's landmark stories that shaped it, spanning multiple categories (politics, economy, tech, weather/climate, legal, sports, culture); do not let a single month dominate.

## Layout
- Landscape, **1536 × 1024** (3:2).
- **One unified montage scene — not panels.** No grid, boxes, panel borders, comic strips, or numbered sections.
- Use a sweeping cohesive editorial environment that reads as a year in one frame: a grand newsroom wall of monitors, a year-end front page come to life, a timeline mural, or a command center. Understandable at a glance despite the story count.

## Content
- Weave all selected stories into one continuous scene as signs, screens, documents, maps, props, crowds, vehicles, weather effects, scoreboards, podiums, charts, or symbolic objects. Balance the density so it stays readable, not cluttered.
- Embed **2–8 word** labels naturally throughout the scene (see Embedded text rules in `../SKILL.md`).

## Title & date
- Place a readable title naturally in the scene (newsroom wall, ticker board, newspaper masthead): **YEAR IN REVIEW**
- Include the exact year: **YYYY**

## Save
- Filename: `Yearly-News-Montage_<YYYY>.png` (use the previous calendar year).
- Save to the output location the user specifies; default to the current working directory and report the full path.

## Email (optional)
- Only if the user asks. Subject: `Yearly News Montage for <YYYY>`
- Plain text: `Attached is the Yearly News Montage for <YYYY>.`
- HTML: `<p>Attached is the <strong>Yearly News Montage</strong> for <YYYY>.</p>`
