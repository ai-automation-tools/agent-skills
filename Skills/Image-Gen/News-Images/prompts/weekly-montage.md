# Weekly News Montage

> Shape: **Montage** (single unified scene, landscape). Read `../SKILL.md` → *Shared conventions* first; this file holds only what's specific to the weekly montage.

Create a single landscape cartoon-style news montage summarizing the **major U.S. news events from the previous completed week**, then save, email, and publish it.

## Period & research
- Resolve the **previous completed Monday–Sunday week** in the user's locale to exact absolute start and end dates; use those throughout the run.
- Research **six to ten** major U.S. news stories from that week via web search. Choose nationally relevant, visually distinct stories representative of the week's politics, economy, weather, technology, legal, sports, health, national events, or cultural stories. Prefer recurring/consequential stories over one-off minor updates.

## Layout
- Landscape, **1536 × 1024** (3:2).
- **One unified montage scene — not panels.** No grid, boxes, panel borders, comic strips, or numbered sections.
- Use a cohesive editorial environment: a chaotic newsroom, command center, map table, city square, control room, or newspaper front page come to life. Understandable at a glance.

## Content
- Weave all selected stories into one continuous scene as signs, screens, documents, maps, props, crowds, vehicles, weather effects, scoreboards, podiums, charts, or symbolic objects.
- Embed **2–8 word** labels naturally throughout the scene (see Embedded text rules in `../SKILL.md`).

## Title & date
- Place a readable title naturally in the scene (newsroom wall, ticker board, newspaper masthead): **WEEK IN REVIEW**
- Include the exact week range: **MMM D-D, YYYY** — if the week crosses months, use **MMM D - MMM D, YYYY**.

## Save
- Filename: `Weekly-News-Montage_<YYYY-MM-DD>_to_<YYYY-MM-DD>.png` (use the week's start/end dates).
- Save to the output location the user specifies; default to the current working directory and report the full path.

## Email (optional)
- Only if the user asks. Subject: `Weekly News Montage for <MMM D-D, YYYY>`
- Plain text: `Attached is the Weekly News Montage for <MMM D-D, YYYY>.`
- HTML: `<p>Attached is the <strong>Weekly News Montage</strong> for <MMM D-D, YYYY>.</p>`
