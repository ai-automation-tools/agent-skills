# Monthly News Collage

> Shape: **Collage** (6-panel grid, portrait). Read `../SKILL.md` → *Shared conventions* first; this file holds only what's specific to the monthly collage.

Create a vertical cartoon-style news collage summarizing the **major U.S. news stories from the previous calendar month**, then save, email, and publish it.

## Period & research
- Resolve the **previous calendar month** in the user's locale; use that exact month and year throughout the run.
- Research **six** major U.S. news stories from that month via web search. Choose nationally relevant, visually distinct stories representative of the month rather than one single day.

## Layout
- Portrait, **1024 × 1536** (2:3), matching the Daily News Collage archive.
- **6 equal square panels, 3 rows × 2 columns**, clean borders between panels. Balanced, readable.
- No extra canvas height, footer, or poster stretch beyond 1024 × 1536.

## Content
- One distinct story per panel; each panel a self-contained mini-scene.
- Each panel carries **3–8 words** of embedded contextual text identifying its story (see Embedded text rules in `../SKILL.md`).

## Timestamp
- Small professional month stamp in the **bottom-right corner** of the whole image (outside the boxes if space allows).
- Format: **Month YYYY**

## Save
- Filename: `Monthly-News-Collage_<Month>_<YYYY>.png` (full month name, e.g. `Monthly-News-Collage_February_2026.png`, not an abbreviation).
- Save to the output location the user specifies; default to the current working directory and report the full path.

## Email (optional)
- Only if the user asks. Subject: `Monthly News Collage for <Month YYYY>`
- Plain text: `Attached is the Monthly News Collage for <Month YYYY>.`
- HTML: `<p>Attached is the <strong>Monthly News Collage</strong> for <Month YYYY>.</p>`
