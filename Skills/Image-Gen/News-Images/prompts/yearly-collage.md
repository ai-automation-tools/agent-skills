# Yearly News Collage

> Shape: **Collage** (6-panel grid, portrait). Read `../SKILL.md` → *Shared conventions* first; this file holds only what's specific to the yearly collage.

Create a vertical cartoon-style news collage summarizing the **defining U.S. news stories of the previous calendar year**, then save, email, and publish it.

## Period & research
- Resolve the **previous full calendar year** (e.g. run in early 2027 → year **2026**); use that exact year throughout the run.
- Research the **six** biggest, most defining U.S. news stories of that year via web search. Choose the year's landmark stories — the ones that dominated or shaped the year — that are nationally relevant and visually distinct. Span multiple categories (politics, economy, tech, weather/climate, legal, sports/culture); do not let a single month dominate.

## Layout
- Portrait, **1024 × 1536** (2:3), matching the collage archive.
- **6 equal square panels, 3 rows × 2 columns**, clean borders between panels. Balanced, readable.
- No extra canvas height, footer, or poster stretch beyond 1024 × 1536.

## Content
- One landmark story per panel; each panel a self-contained mini-scene evoking the whole arc of that story, not a single moment.
- Each panel carries **3–8 words** of embedded contextual text identifying its story (see Embedded text rules in `../SKILL.md`).

## Timestamp
- Small professional year stamp in the **bottom-right corner** of the whole image (outside the boxes if space allows).
- Format: **YYYY**

## Save
- Filename: `Yearly-News-Collage_<YYYY>.png` (use the previous calendar year).
- Save to the output location the user specifies; default to the current working directory and report the full path.

## Email (optional)
- Only if the user asks. Subject: `Yearly News Collage for <YYYY>`
- Plain text: `Attached is the Yearly News Collage for <YYYY>.`
- HTML: `<p>Attached is the <strong>Yearly News Collage</strong> for <YYYY>.</p>`
